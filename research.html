Dr. Singisetti Research Group — Research Trainee

Sep 2026 – present · University at Buffalo

Weekly mentored training in semiconductor device characterization, learning techniques from lab members in the group. The work so far is hands-on measurement: probing generic samples to check linearity and ohmic properties, and testing FET IV curves — measuring how a device actually behaves, then checking whether that matches what was expected, even when it doesn't match the first guess.

This is trainee-level characterization training, not device design or production work, and the samples I have tested were generic — the group's wider area is ultrawide-bandgap power and RF devices, which is not the same as claiming I tested those materials myself.
