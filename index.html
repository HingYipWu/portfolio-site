    About me

    I'm Hing Yip Wu, an Electrical Engineering student at the University at Buffalo on a three-year B.S. track (May 2027), continuing into an M.S. (May 2028). I got into EE through hands-on work — first in a wireless systems lab, now as an intern in electrical design and as a trainee in semiconductors — and I'm using personal projects to figure out which part of the field fits me best, rather than picking a label first.

    Right now that means learning how devices actually behave: probing samples, measuring IV curves, and checking whether what I measure matches what I expected. I like finding the real result, even when it doesn't match the first guess.

    This site is where I post the projects I build myself, as I build them. Outside of school and work, I usually clear my head on bike rides.
